Our Services —   Advisory Services
CaliberCode provides end-to-end operational, compliance, and governance advisory directly to client organizations. We handle technical execution, regulatory interface, and risk mitigation so client leadership can focus on core growth.
1. Statutory & Tax Compliance
We support businesses in navigating Kenyan tax laws and statutory mandates through hands-on compliance support, filing assistance, and proactive risk mitigation.
•	KRA Registration & Setup: NEW PIN Registrations & Details Amendments.
•	Obligation Applications: VAT, Turnover Tax (TOT), Excise Duty, and relevant business tax obligations.
•	Direct Tax Returns: Filing Income Tax (Individual & Corporate), Turnover Tax (TOT), and Monthly Rental Income (MRI) Corporate Tax.
•	Indirect Tax & Operations: VAT Compliance & Returns, PAYE, and eTIMS setup and management.
•	Statutory Payroll Compliance: Full compliance management for NSSF, SHA (Social Health Authority), Affordable Housing Levy, and Tourism Fund.
•	Statutory Health Checks: Pre-audit reviews, statutory records management, and comprehensive compliance gap analysis.
•	Deadline Management: Customized corporate compliance calendars engineered to eliminate late fees, penalties, and interest.

2. Governance, Risk & Internal Audit Advisory
•	Corporate Governance: Evaluating board structures, committee effectiveness, and fiduciary governance practices.
•	Enterprise Risk Management: Designing and embedding ERM frameworks tailored to industry-specific risk landscapes.
•	Internal Audit Execution: Establishing risk-based internal audit functions, audit charters, and execution plans.
•	Policy & Control Frameworks: Developing, reviewing, and updating organizational policies, SOPs, and control frameworks.

3. Fraud, Financial Crime & Digital Asset Advisory
•	Fraud Risk Advisory: Designing proactive anti-fraud controls, fraud risk assessments, and reporting mechanisms.
•	AML/CFT Compliance: Implementing Anti-Money Laundering (AML), Countering the Financing of Terrorism (CFT), and Know Your Customer (KYC) protocols.
•	Whistleblowing & Response: Establishing secure whistleblowing channels, incident investigation, and response frameworks.
•	Digital Asset & Data Advisory: Assessing organizational posture against data protection laws and virtual asset regulations.
